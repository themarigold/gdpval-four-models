**File:** SCRA_3937_3919_Audit_Test_Questions.xlsx

**Structure**
- **Test Questions tab:** One question per row, with the test ID, statute, pinpoint citation, topic, question, response options (Yes / No / Not Applicable), compliant-response and exception criteria, and audit evidence to review. The Auditor Response column has a Yes / No / Not Applicable dropdown, and there is a notes column for workpaper references.
- **Instructions & Key tab:** Response key, assumptions, and a coverage map that ties each statutory paragraph to a test ID. It also notes what is not tested and how to add tests in future.

**Design choices**
- **Yes means compliant in every row.** Prohibitions are phrased as "Did the servicer avoid…", so a Yes is always the favorable answer.
- **§3937 coverage:** 12a covers the 6% cap during service, 12b the one-year mortgage extension, 12c forgiveness of excess interest and payment reduction (fees count as interest), and 12d the notice trigger and retroactive effective date, including the DMDC alternative.
- **§3919 coverage:** Each of the six items maps to one test. §3919(2)(A)–(B) is combined in SCRA-14, and §3919(4) and (6) are combined in SCRA-17, to keep one test per ID.
- **§3919 trigger:** The tests apply only where the borrower requested or received SCRA relief. Because the statute says the relief cannot be the basis for the listed actions "without regard to other considerations," the key treats documented, independent reasons as non-exceptions, but the auditor should confirm those reasons are genuine.
