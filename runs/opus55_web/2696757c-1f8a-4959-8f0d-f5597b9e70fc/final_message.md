The VA link you gave wouldn't load for me (the site requires JavaScript). I worked from a mirrored copy of Chapter 9, so it's worth checking the exact wording against the official PDF before the test is used. The two paragraphs say:
- **9.07(a)(2)(a):** If the borrower files Chapter 13 bankruptcy during the trial payment plan, the servicer must provide evidence that the bankruptcy court approved or accepted the VASP. If the borrower files Chapter 7, they don't qualify for VASP until the bankruptcy is closed.
- **9.08(c)(3):** If the borrower's VA-guaranteed loan debt was discharged in Chapter 7, the VASP loan modification document must include VA's set bankruptcy statement.

The test questions:
1. **9.07(a)(2)(a):** Where the borrower filed bankruptcy during the trial payment plan, did the servicer meet the condition for that type of bankruptcy before going ahead with the VASP? For Chapter 13, that means evidence of court approval or acceptance. For Chapter 7, it means holding off until the bankruptcy was closed.
2. **9.08(c)(3):** Where the debt was discharged in Chapter 7, did the servicer include VA's required statement in the VASP loan modification document? The statement says the agreement creates no personal liability, is not a reaffirmation of the debt, and is not an attempt to collect it.

Decisions you may want to revisit:
- **Question 1 covers both Chapter 13 and Chapter 7**, because the paragraph has a separate rule for each. Its exception statement has a bracketed sentence for each chapter, and the tester keeps the one that applies.
- **The second exception statement also uses bracketed options**, one for a missing statement and one for an incomplete or altered one.
- **The exception statements mention possible consequences:** VA post-audit findings, enforcement action under 38 C.F.R. § 36.4320, and, for the discharge statement, the bankruptcy discharge injunction (11 U.S.C. § 524). These go beyond the two paragraphs themselves, so remove them if your reporting standards limit findings to the cited requirement.
