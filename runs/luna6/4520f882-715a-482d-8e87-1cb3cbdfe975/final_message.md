The workbook includes:
- **START HERE** — workflow, color key, assumptions, and notes from the sample roster and schedule.
- **SETUP & RATES / RATES** — weekly setup, the excerpt’s 2025 rates, and editable slots for future annual rate updates.
- **ROSTER** — 60 musician slots, including inactive sample instrument slots, premium eligibility inputs, doubles, and audit-book entries.
- **SERVICE LOG** — 1,000 rows for per-musician service entries, with formulas and CBA review flags.
- **PAYROLL SUMMARY** — per-person totals for performance/guarantee pay, rehearsals, sound checks, premiums, doubling, substitute audit pay, vacation pay, and gross pay.

The workbook flags items such as service dates outside the payroll week, rehearsal minimum-call or schedule issues, sound-check duration/window issues, roster conflicts, and premium overrides. Sound-check frequency requires contractor confirmation because it depends on engagement history beyond a single weekly log.

**Assumptions to review:** performance pay uses the weekly guarantee as a floor when enabled on the roster; overlapping premium categories use the highest indicated rate rather than stacking because the excerpt does not specify stacking; and situations omitted from the excerpt should be checked against the full CBA before payment.
