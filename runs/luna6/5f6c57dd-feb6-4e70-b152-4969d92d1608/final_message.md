The workbook includes five reporting tabs—Income Statement, Trend 2024, Branch Ranking, Region Compare, and KPI Metrics—plus the source data tab. Each reporting tab has an **All Company / branch dropdown**.

The ranking tab keeps all 10 branches visible and highlights the selected branch. The regional tab keeps Regions A–G visible and updates its selected branch/company summary. Regional figures use branch-assigned activity; because the source records shared-service allocations under Corporate, regional allocation figures are zero, while the All Company view includes Corporate costs and allocations.

Expense increases are shown as unfavorable variances. The KPI tab includes the calculation basis for headcount and backlog measures.
