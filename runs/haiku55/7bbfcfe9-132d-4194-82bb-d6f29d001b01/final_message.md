I've built the workbook with ten test questions: SCRA-12a through 12d for §3937 and SCRA-13 through 18 for §3919. I could not open the govinfo.gov links from the sandbox, so the questions are drafted from my knowledge of the statutes. Please check the subsection citations and the §3919 wording against the official text before the audit.

**File:** `SCRA_3937_3919_Audit_Test_Questions.xlsx`

**Sheet 1, "SCRA Test Questions"** has one row per question with these columns:
- Test ID
- Statute
- Citation
- Subject
- Test Question
- Response (dropdown limited to Yes / No / Not Applicable)
- Use "Not Applicable" When
- Auditor Notes / Evidence Reviewed

**Questions covered:**
- **SCRA-12a (§3937(a)(1)):** The servicer correctly identified the mortgage as a pre-service obligation subject to the 6% limit.
- **SCRA-12b (§3937(a)(1)):** The 6% limit was applied for the period of military service and one year after.
- **SCRA-12c (§3937(a)(1)):** Interest above 6% was forgiven, not billed, capitalized, added to principal, or collected.
- **SCRA-12d (§3937(b)):** After written notice and a copy of orders, the 6% limit covered the full covered period, not just from the notice date.
- **SCRA-13 (§3919):** No adverse credit reporting resulted from the exercise of SCRA rights.
- **SCRA-14 (§3919):** Any adverse credit information was corrected and the consumer reporting agencies were notified.
- **SCRA-15 (§3919):** The exercise of SCRA rights was not a negative factor in later loss mitigation, modification, refinance, or other credit decisions.
- **SCRA-16 (§3919):** No negative notations or risk/hardship flags were placed on the account because of SCRA rights.
- **SCRA-17 (§3919):** Later financial transactions were processed under the same standards as for borrowers who did not exercise SCRA rights.
- **SCRA-18 (§3919):** The servicer followed its written procedures that bar using SCRA rights in credit reporting or future transaction decisions.

**Sheet 2, "Response Key & Notes"** defines the responses and lists the assumptions. For the testing, "Yes" means compliant, "No" means an exception, and "Not Applicable" applies only when the stated condition is met.
