[Download the weekly musician payroll workbook](sandbox:/mnt/data/outputs/Weekly_Musician_Payroll_Model.xlsx)

It includes **Start Here, Rates, Roster, Service Log, Payroll Summary, and Sample Reference** tabs. The summary calculates each musician’s performance rates, rehearsal pay, sound-check pay, audit pay, premiums, doubling, vacation pay, and gross total. Contractor inputs are highlighted, with review flags for potential CBA conflicts.

The 2025 rates from the excerpt are preloaded; the effective-date rate schedule can be updated for later years. The workbook also documents assumptions for provisions that are ambiguous or cannot be verified from a single weekly payroll file.
