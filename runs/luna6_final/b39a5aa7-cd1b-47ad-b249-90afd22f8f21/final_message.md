The workbook includes a dashboard with 2026 quarterly compensation expense by type, quarterly projections and year-over-year growth for 2027–2028, editable CBA and roster inputs, and employee-level calculation tabs. The original **Assumptions** and **Roster** tabs are retained.

**Important modeling assumption:** The source specifies 39 media weeks but not the paid weeks for other weekly compensation. The model defaults other weekly pay to the same 39-week schedule; adjust the quarterly week inputs if the actual schedule differs.
